If you’re still running your stock on spreadsheets, you already know the problem. Someone updates one file and forgets to update another. A production run kicks off and you’re halfway through before anyone realises a critical component ran out last Tuesday. A supplier chases you for a purchase order and the person who placed it is on holiday.

Spreadsheets were never designed for this. And the bigger your operation gets, the more painfully obvious that becomes.

This is why manufacturers across the UK and beyond are switching to dedicated inventory management software for manufacturing — not generic business tools bolted together, but systems built for how production environments actually work.

Here is a practical look at what that switch involves and what good inventory control looks like in a real manufacturing business.

## What is inventory management software for manufacturing?

Inventory management software for manufacturing is a system that tracks your stock levels, movements, and requirements in real time — connected directly to your sales orders, production schedule, and purchasing activity.

Unlike a stock management bolt-on or a standalone spreadsheet, it is part of your broader [manufacturing ERP software]({{ '/purchasing-inventory-software/' | relative_url }}). That connection matters. When a sales order is confirmed, the system already knows what components are needed, what you have in stock, and what needs to be ordered. There is no manual reconciliation across separate files or systems.

In a make-to-order environment especially, that visibility is the difference between hitting your delivery dates and constantly firefighting.

## Why spreadsheets break down at scale

Spreadsheets are not inherently bad. They are flexible, familiar, and free. The problem is that manufacturing inventory is a live, fast-moving dataset that gets updated by multiple people, across multiple shifts, against multiple active jobs — and spreadsheets are not built for that.

Here is what tends to go wrong:

- **Version conflicts** — two people update the same file at different times and the latest version is not always the correct one
- **No real-time visibility** — someone books stock out at 7am but production planning does not know until they check the file manually at 10am
- **Single points of failure** — the person who maintains the stock file leaves, and nobody else knows how it works
- **No alerts** — you only find out a component has run out when production stops

These are not edge cases. They are the everyday reality for most manufacturers who have outgrown spreadsheets but have not yet moved to a proper system.

## What good inventory control looks like in manufacturing

When cloud ERP for manufacturing is set up properly, inventory stops being a problem you react to and becomes something you manage proactively.

The key changes are straightforward.

### Real-time stock visibility

Every transaction — goods in, goods out, allocations to production jobs, scrap — updates the system immediately. Anyone in the business can see current stock levels without having to ask someone or wait for a file to be updated.

This feeds directly into purchasing decisions and production planning. If your MRP (material requirements planning) is running off accurate, real-time inventory data, your purchase orders go out at the right time — not after you have already missed a delivery date.

### Mobile stock takes that actually get done

One of the biggest barriers to accurate inventory is the stock take itself. Traditional stock takes are disruptive. You either stop production to count everything, or you try to count around running operations and end up with data you do not fully trust.

With Fraction ERP, shop floor staff can carry out stock takes on the fly using their mobile device — no paper, no desk, no reconciliation process afterwards. Counts update in real time. The whole process becomes something that happens as part of the normal working day rather than a quarterly emergency.

This is not a minor convenience. It is the thing that makes your inventory data trustworthy day to day.

### Bulk stock updates when you need them

There are times when you need to manipulate stock data in bulk — an initial data load when you first go live, a mass correction after a physical audit, or a category-wide update. Fraction ERP lets you export your stock data to a spreadsheet, make the changes you need, and import it back. Clean, practical, and controlled.

It is a useful escape hatch. The system does not force you into a rigid workflow when a bulk edit is simply the faster, more sensible approach.

### Stock alerts linked to your daily management routine

Setting minimum stock levels is straightforward in any decent system. What makes the difference is what happens when those levels are breached.

In Fraction ERP, stock alerts surface on the main dashboard — the same screen your team checks at the start of every day. If you run a short daily operations check-in (what some teams call a sunrise meeting — a focused seven-minute standup to review priorities and flag issues), stock alerts become part of that rhythm rather than a separate task that gets overlooked.

Monitoring key components becomes habitual. Problems surface early, not when production has already stopped.

This is how production scheduling software and inventory control work together properly. You are not managing stock in isolation — you are managing it as part of your daily operation.

Done properly, it eliminates the manual work of calculating material needs for each job — and the risk of getting those calculations wrong.
