An ERP system is designed to help you run your business. But, when our performance isn’t quite where it needs to be it is easy to take our eye off the ball. And, as we all know, taking our said eye off the said ball doesn’t lead us to where we want to be.

If we want the results that our business can deliver, we need to focus on the key information, however bad it is currently, and persist whilst taking the right actions. The Fraction ERP dashboard is one great way to do this.

## Make the best decisions possible

Seeing the real, and current, status of key processes and activities in a business is paramount if we want to make the best decisions possible. Leadership is all about making decisions, ideally good ones. That’s why we developed the dashboard element of Fraction ERP the way we did.

Each tile on the dashboard allows you to drill down into the data behind it, allowing you and your team to make informed decisions. The better the data, the higher chance you have of making a good decision.

If you have studied key performance indicators (KPIs), you’ll know the distinction between leading and lagging indicators. The dashboard is primarily leading indicators, so you have a chance to change the outcome (rather than reading about a disaster after it has happened).

## What this means in the real world

Some examples from Fraction ERP’s dashboard include:

- **Stock alerts**. If you review this on a daily basis, attempting to stay at zero stock alerts, you have the opportunity to raise a purchase order, replenish stock and this can lead to on time delivery of your orders.
- **Late works orders**. By constantly nudging the slipping works orders you have the best chance of keeping the overall schedule on track. On track orders leads to high on time delivery performance, fewer customer complaints and better repeat business.
- **Parts for inspection**. Keeping this metric to zero helps to ensure that all stock is moving in your business, towards production, and out of the door. Keeping material flowing helps to minimise the amount of time you have to spend revising your production schedules.

## Pull this together with your team

So, whether you are using Fraction ERP or not, please use your ERP data to keep track of all the key numbers in your business.

Holding a sunrise meeting, using an ERP dashboard as the agenda, is a great way to ensure that your team are focused on the things that count and to keep your ERP system data clean and usable.

If you cringe when you see your KPIs, we urge you to detach your feelings from the scores. Use the information as information only, not a judgement on you. Figure out what the information is telling you and then determine the best course of action.

It’s as simple as that!
