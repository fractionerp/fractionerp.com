Our Story

Where Fraction came from

Fraction began as a production-management tool created to solve practical planning problems inside a precision engineering company. Its first deployment increased output by 10%, raised on-time delivery from 50% to 90% and reduced overdue orders by 95%.

That working foundation was developed into a complete ERP system for small and mid-sized manufacturers. The aim was not to copy enterprise software on a smaller scale, but to provide the control manufacturers need with clearer workflows and less overhead.

How Fraction develops today

Fraction is not treated as a finished product. It is continuously refined through two constant sources of information: our own daily experience running manufacturing and feedback from Fraction customers.

When we find duplicated information, unnecessary administration or workflow friction, we examine the process and improve the product. Customer experience then adds a wider range of manufacturing requirements to that development loop.
