Manufacturing ERP, built by people who run a factory

Fraction was founded in 2019 by Patrick Chester, who is also managing director of Oiltech Bearings, a UK precision engineering business. Oiltech still runs on Fraction every day.

Book a Demo Take the ERP assessment

Our story

In 2019, Patrick started Fraction to fix the chasing, re-keying and spreadsheets in a working machine shop. The starting point was the work itself: keeping orders, materials and production connected.

Fraction launched to other manufacturers in 2021. What began inside a precision engineering business developed into an ERP system covering sales, purchasing, stock, production, quality, shipping and invoicing.

Today, about 35 UK manufacturers use Fraction, and revenue has roughly doubled each year. Oiltech continues to use the same system every day, so development stays connected to running a factory as well as feedback from our customers.

Who you’ll deal with

We’re a small UK team in Newcastle. The people who build Fraction also answer support, so you can talk to people who know the product and understand manufacturing.

Online and telephone support is unlimited on every plan. We’ll help you work through questions about your orders, stock, scheduling and day-to-day use of the system.
