Fraction ERP vs Statii

Comparing a Statii alternative for your manufacturing business? Start with the jobs your team needs to manage, the people using the system and the total subscription cost.

Statii positions its MRP software for small and medium make-to-order manufacturers. Fraction ERP connects sales, MRP, purchasing, stock, production and delivery in one cloud system. There is substantial overlap, so a useful comparison goes beyond a feature checklist.

Where Fraction ERP belongs on your shortlist

Consider the wider operation as well as your immediate production-control needs.

Explore how Fraction connects orders, MRP, stock, scheduling, production and fulfilment. The manufacturing feature directory shows the workflow and available modules.

Shop-floor access as the team grows

Included SFDC users make the operator count worth discussing. Decide which people only need SFDC and which require standard access to purchasing, planning, management or other modules.

Broader control where you need it

Requirements such as controlled SOPs, production inspection routines or bar and offcut management deserve their own demonstration and plan check.

Keep the Statii evaluation specific too

Statii's published make-to-order focus and broad main-user access may fit your requirements. Test its job workflow, role permissions and reporting using the same examples you give Fraction. Ask Statii to demonstrate any additional requirements outside the scope of this comparison.

Give both suppliers the same demonstration brief

A familiar job with real exceptions tells you more than a polished example that never changes.

1. Quote and revise a real job

Use your materials, operations and subcontract requirements. Change the quantity after quoting and follow the effect on costs, demand and documents.

2. Schedule around a constraint

Load work onto your bottleneck, remove an operator's availability and add an urgent order. Check operation sequence, capacity and delivery dates.

3. Handle a partial receipt

Receive six items against an order for ten. Follow the outstanding balance, stock availability and effect on the works order.

4. Close the loop on quality and cost

Record a defect, attach evidence and book rework time. Check how the issue affects stock, delivery and the final job-cost review.

Switching from Statii? Start with your data and processes

Agree what needs to move before choosing a go-live date.

List the customer, supplier, part, BOM, stock and open-order records you need to carry forward. Confirm export formats and any charges with your current supplier, then agree the import scope with Fraction. Decide how completed-job history and supporting documents will remain accessible.

Validate sample records, opening balances and a representative order before planning the changeover. Include the people who quote, buy, schedule and book production in that review.
