About Trinity Precision

High-end machining with quality at the forefront.

Trinity Precision Engineering is a quality-first machining specialist serving customers across motorsport, aerospace, and defence. From its North East England facility, the team produces high-precision components using advanced CNC machinery, including 5-axis machining and automated pallet loading.

Its capabilities span CNC milling and turning, CAD/CAM, laser marking, and quality inspection. A commitment to accuracy, traceability, and customer service is backed by ISO 9001:2015 certification.

The Challenge

An unreliable ERP system was holding the office team back.

Trinity already had an ERP system in place. But E-Max Systems created persistent reliability problems for the office team. Instead of helping work move smoothly, the system added friction to everyday processes and became a constant source of frustration.

Replacing an existing ERP is a significant decision. Trinity decided that the unreliable system was holding the business back and made the move to Fraction.

Carl Buckley on the previous ERP

“Before switching to Fraction ERP, our office team had a constant headache due to the problems with our last ERP system — the lack of a reliable system was holding us back.”

Carl Buckley — Managing Director, Trinity Precision Engineering

The Solution

A dependable ERP replacement, proven in everyday work.

Fraction replaced E-Max Systems as Trinity's ERP. Materials ordering and supplier RFQs are two areas Carl Buckley singled out for the difference they made.

Effortless materials ordering

Clear, intuitive purchasing workflows help the team order the materials production needs without unnecessary complexity or delays.

Streamlined supplier RFQs

RFQs are easier to prepare and manage, saving office time and making the purchasing process more straightforward.

For Trinity, these practical improvements are part of a wider change: moving the office team from an unreliable ERP to Fraction.

Customer Perspective

“We're especially impressed by how effortlessly Fraction handles materials ordering and RFQs — it's intuitive, streamlined, and saves us time. Fraction ERP hasn't just improved our workflow — it's transformed the way we work.”

Carl Buckley — Managing Director, Trinity Precision Engineering
