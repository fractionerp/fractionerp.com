Familiar tools. A growing business.

Spreadsheets are how many manufacturing businesses start. They're flexible, familiar and quick to put together. For a while, they can be exactly what's needed.

But as the business grows, something often changes. The spreadsheets that once helped organise the business gradually become the system holding it together.

The question isn't whether spreadsheets are good or bad.

Are they still helping your business run — or has your business started working around them?
