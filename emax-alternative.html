Fraction ERP vs E-Max

Which manufacturing ERP is right for you?

Fraction ERP and E-Max are both UK systems for engineering and manufacturing SMEs. E-Max Systems is based in Scotland and offers cloud, on-premise or hybrid deployment. Fraction is cloud only, built in Newcastle and used every day at Oiltech Bearings, an affiliated precision engineering company.

Key differences

Deployment: cloud is not the dividing line

E-Max offers cloud, on-premise or hybrid deployment. Fraction is cloud only. If you must use your own servers, E-Max offers that option; if cloud suits you, compare fit, costs and customisation.

E-Max describes automatic updates for cloud deployments. Confirm infrastructure and maintenance responsibilities for an on-premise proposal. Compare E-Max deployment options.

Implementation time

Fraction is designed to go live in weeks, often without consultants, with unlimited online training included.

E-Max’s cloud guide describes faster deployment in weeks in its benefits section, but gives 6–9 months for a full SME implementation elsewhere. These are general statements, not a schedule for your project. Give both suppliers a sample job and target date, and agree migration and training scope in writing. Read the E-Max cloud guide.

Quality, SOPs and training records

E-Max publishes document control for SOPs, inspections, non-conformance, calibration and traceability. Its October 2025 training-platform article described assignments and completion tracking as planned; ask for a demonstration of their current availability.

Fraction includes NCRs on every plan, certificates of conformance on Advanced, and inspection reports, SOPs and the training log on Pro. Serial and batch traceability are on Advanced and Pro. E-Max quality management · Fraction plan details.

Customisation and fit

E-Max offers bespoke reports, API links and custom add-ons shaped around customer processes. That is a reason to shortlist it when you need a configurable system.

Fraction’s capabilities expand through Core, Advanced and Pro, with published extras such as Time & Attendance and the Moulding Edition. Compare the workflow and plan you actually need. E-Max product overview.

Costs outside the subscription

Fraction’s optional Time & Attendance add-on is £3 per user per month. On-site training is £600 a day plus travel. Online training and telephone support are included. View published pricing.

Ask E-Max for a like-for-like quote

Include five office users, ten operators, deployment, modules, implementation, training and support. E-Max’s cloud guide gives a general SME market range, not an E-Max price list. Compare a written proposal with Fraction’s published figures over the same period.

When to choose each system

Choose Fraction ERP if you:

  • Want published monthly prices and unlimited free shop-floor users
  • Are happy with cloud-only deployment
  • Want telephone support and online training included
  • Need SOPs and a training log in the same system (Fraction Pro)
  • Prefer a smaller, newer UK supplier that runs the software in its own workshop

Choose E-Max if you:

  • Need on-premise or hybrid deployment, not only cloud
  • Want a configurable system, with bespoke reports or custom add-ons, from a supplier that has been in this market since 1998
  • Need the breadth of quality and compliance tools described on E-Max's quality page
  • Want a UK supplier with a long customer base in engineering SMEs
  • Are prepared to get a written proposal rather than pick a plan off a website

Why companies look for E-Max alternatives

E-Max serves UK engineering and manufacturing SMEs with cloud and on-premise options. Consider alternatives when your priorities differ.

  • You want published monthly prices before booking a demo.
  • You want shop-floor users included in the subscription.
  • You prefer published plans to a more configurable project.
  • You do not need on-premise deployment and want to agree a manageable go-live scope.
