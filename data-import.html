Prepare your data for the move

Use the import templates as part of a planned changeover from spreadsheets or an existing ERP system.

1. Gather your existing records

Identify the customers, suppliers, parts, BOMs and current orders you need to carry forward. If you are leaving another ERP system, arrange the exports you need from that supplier.

2. Prepare the Excel templates

Put your records into the relevant templates. Use this preparation to review your data and make sure it reflects what your team needs at changeover.

3. Import and review with your team

Use the self-service tool to import your prepared data. Review the imported records with the people who will use them before making the system part of everyday work.

Plan around the work you already have

Your current sales order book and purchase orders matter when changing systems. Include those commitments in your migration preparation so the team can review the work being carried forward.
